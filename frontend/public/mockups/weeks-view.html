Key invariants tested by this mockup:

  • Total MM is identical in both views (Solution Arch = 5.50 MM · SAP FICO = 5.50 MM · QA = 4.00 MM).
  • Salary Cost / Overhead / Selling Price / SP/MM / Hourly are unchanged when switching modes.
  • The "Add Month" button label switches to "Add Week"; adding one week extends duration by 0.25 MM.
  • Phase Ranges stay in months → Gantt chart, cashflow, milestones need zero changes.
  • Weeks view will be capped at waves ≤ 6 months (24 columns) to avoid horizontal-scroll fatigue.
